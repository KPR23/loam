Delete Your Account

You can delete your Loam account and data directly in the app.

  • Open Loam and go to Profile → Personal details → Delete account.
  • Confirm with Sign in with Apple.
  • Access to your account closes immediately. Deletion usually finishes within a few minutes, and the app shows when it’s complete.

What gets deleted

Your profile, display name, profile photo, private expenses, recurring expenses, connections and any usage analytics. Your Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked.

Expenses you shared with other people remain visible to them so their balances stay correct, with your name shown as a deleted user.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Loam Pro subscription. Cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Can’t access the app?

Email kprcode@icloud.com from the address linked to your Apple Account and we’ll help you delete your account.
