Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Loam (“the App”) is provided by Kacper Zabłudowski / KPR's Lab (“we”), who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: kprcode@icloud.com.

We collect only what the App needs to track your spending and shared balances. We do not sell personal data, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist on our website, your email address, when you joined and, if present, the link that brought you there.
  • Account. When you sign in with Apple we receive a unique Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address (which may be an Apple private relay address).
  • Profile. The display name you set and, optionally, a profile photo you pick from your photo library. Only the photo you select is uploaded.
  • Expenses. Amounts, currencies, categories, titles, notes, dates, recurring schedules and, if you add one, a place (name, address and coordinates).
  • Shared balances. Your connections with other Loam users, expenses you split with them, their acceptance of those splits, repayments, and the related activity history.
  • Location. With your permission, your current location is used on your device to suggest nearby places when adding an expense. We do not store your location; only a place you choose is saved with the expense.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, Apple issues a push token for your device. We store it with your account, together with your notification choices, only to deliver alerts about your Activity. Turn them off in Profile or iOS Settings, or sign out, to stop them.
  • Purchases. Your Loam Pro subscription status and purchase history. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details.
  • Crash reports. Technical diagnostics when the App crashes or hits an error (device model, iOS version, app version, stack traces), linked to your account identifier.
  • Usage analytics (optional, off by default). Only if you turn on “Share usage analytics” in Profile. Analytics are linked to your account identifier and include which screens you open, app open/close events, events such as “expense created” (with whether it was shared or recurring and how many participants), and session recordings in which all text, inputs and images are masked on your device. Analytics never include amounts, descriptions, names or locations.

Why we use it

  • To provide the App: store and sync your expenses, calculate balances and share split expenses with the people you connect with (performance of our contract with you).
  • To provide and verify Loam Pro (performance of contract).
  • To find and fix crashes and errors (our legitimate interest in a reliable app).
  • To understand how the App is used and improve it, only if you opt in (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Profile → Privacy).
  • To invite you to test early versions of Loam through Apple’s TestFlight, if you joined the waitlist (your consent). Email kprcode@icloud.com to be removed at any time.
  • To answer your support emails.

Who can see your data

Your private expenses are visible only to you. When you split an expense with a connection, that person sees the shared expense, your display name and profile photo, and your balance with them.

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — account, database, file storage and the waitlist.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting (EU region).
  • PostHog — optional usage analytics (EU region).
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, Apple Maps place search and TestFlight invitations.

To convert expenses in other currencies, the App downloads daily European Central Bank exchange rates from Frankfurter, a free public service. These requests ask only for the rates of a date range and include no account details or expense data; like any website, the service can see your device’s IP address.

“Give feedback” in Profile opens our feedback board on Featurebase, a separate service with its own privacy policy. Anything you post there is handled by Featurebase, not stored in the App.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, profile photo, private expenses and analytics data are deleted, and your Apple sign-in is revoked. Expenses you shared with other people stay visible to them so their balances remain correct, with your name replaced by a “deleted user” label. Crash reports expire automatically under Sentry’s retention period. Purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat as required for accounting and tax purposes.

We keep your waitlist email until you ask us to remove it or we stop running early access. Apple keeps TestFlight tester details under its own privacy policy.

See Delete Your Account for details.

Your rights

You can access and edit your data in the App and delete your account at any time from Profile → Personal details → Delete account. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, correction, restriction, portability, or to object to processing. Email kprcode@icloud.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU, you may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO).

Children

Loam is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the App.
