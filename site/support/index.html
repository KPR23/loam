Common questions

How do I share expenses with someone?
Connect with them using a connection code from the app, then add an expense and split it with them. They review the split before it counts toward your balance.

How do I restore Loam Pro on a new device?
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Loam Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel Loam Pro?
Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone and cancel Loam Pro. See Apple’s guide.

How do I request a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Profile → Personal details → Delete account. More details.
